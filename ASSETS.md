## Sound
Built by `audio/make.sh` from `assets-cache/sounds/` and `assets-cache/kenney/` (ffmpeg: mono 32 kHz Ogg Vorbis, trimmed, loops crossfaded seamless at -20 LUFS, one-shots at a -3 dBFS peak). `<name>.ogg` is the clear copy (inside, full air), `<name>_out.ogg` the same sound low-passed (900 Hz, twice) for Mars through a helmet: a web build cannot run bus filters, so the muffle is baked in. Footsteps on sand and rock are low-passed at 450 Hz with a bass lift: a boot heard through the suit, not the air. The 900 Hz cutoff is a guess by ear, not a sourced number. 43 files, 516 KB.

### Music
Rare by design (`design/research/alive.md` section 5): a cue at a few earned moments, silence the rest of the time. Built by `bash audio/make.sh music` from `assets-cache/music/` (Ravindu's picks, its README "Picked by Ravindu"): stereo 44.1 kHz Ogg Vorbis, about -16 LUFS under a -1.5 dBFS limiter, and **not muffled** (music is not a sound in the Mars air). Played on the `Music` bus by `audio.music(name)`; level, fade and the least gap between cues in `data/tuning.json` `audio.music`. 6 files, 668 KB. Not shipped yet: Cleyton Kauffman's "Exploration Theme" and The Cynic Project's "Lifewave 2k", the title and bed picks, until a title screen exists (about 5 MB of music no scene plays).
