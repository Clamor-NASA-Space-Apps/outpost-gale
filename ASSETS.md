## Sound
Built by `audio/make.sh` from `assets-cache/sounds/` and `assets-cache/kenney/` (ffmpeg: mono 32 kHz Ogg Vorbis, trimmed, loops crossfaded seamless at -20 LUFS, one-shots at a -3 dBFS peak). `<name>.ogg` is the clear copy (inside, full air), `<name>_out.ogg` the same sound low-passed (900 Hz, twice) for Mars through a helmet: a web build cannot run bus filters, so the muffle is baked in. Footsteps on sand and rock are low-passed at 450 Hz with a bass lift: a boot heard through the suit, not the air. The 900 Hz cutoff is a guess by ear, not a sourced number. 43 files, 516 KB.
