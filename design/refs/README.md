# Reference board

Ravindu collected these 13 images on 12 Nov 2026. **Confirmed with Ravindu on 12 Nov 2026**, image by image, in the factory interview. Rows he corrected say so.

**His rule for every shape (from refs 9 and 11):** take how a reference *looks*, never a shape that cannot exist on Mars. Every rock or landform asset needs a real Gale photo as its shape reference before it is modelled: layered mudstone, sandstone slabs, buttes, boulders, small craters, pebbles, dark dune ripples. No arches, hoodoos, trees or canyon spires.

The images are third-party screenshots and renders, so they are gitignored. Only this file is committed. They must never ship in the game or appear in the submission.

Palettes come from `node ../../../../tools/ref-palette.mjs . --colors 6` (run from this folder). The AVIF files need converting first: `ffmpeg -i 7.avif 7.jpg`.

## What the board says, in one paragraph (confirmed)
A warm, nearly monochrome Mars of faceted rock and sand. Atmosphere does the work: a low sun, haze that dissolves the distance into the sky, and glow on the bright parts. Chunky bevelled machines with big labels. One cool accent colour (cyan or teal) for "on", "interactive" and the HUD. Nothing realistic in texture, everything real in shape, colour and scale. Refs 10 (*Journey*) and 12 (*Astroneer*) carry the most weight; 9 and 11 lend their surface only. Serious, not cartoonish (Ravindu dropped 5.jpeg for that). Ref 8 is the trap to avoid.
