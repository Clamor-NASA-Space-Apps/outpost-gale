Albedo of bright (dusty) vs dark ground:
> "It is important to note that all of the three Mars landings have occurred in relatively bright regions. The albedos of the Viking 1, Viking 2, and Pathfinder sites are 0.27, 0.25, and 0.22, respectively [Pleskot and Miner, 1981], whereas the albedo of typical dark regions is <0.15 [Pleskot and Miner, 1981]. Therefore it is likely that the surface rocks and sands in the dark regions are less coated by bright dust than those seen at any site visited to date."
