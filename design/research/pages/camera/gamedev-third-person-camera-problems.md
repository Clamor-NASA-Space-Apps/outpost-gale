# Third person camera view in games: a record of the most common problems in modern games, solutions taken from new and retro games (Game Developer, community blog)

Fetched 14 Nov 2026 (WebFetch, quoted passages; the author's observations of shipped games)

- Assassin's Creed Syndicate: "the camera often bounces off objects and walls, which can irritate the player"
- Dark Souls 3: "bounces off the walls in tight environments"
- Elder Scrolls Online, at a doorway: "the camera will bounce off the other door, which could frighten the player"
- For Honor: "There the camera can fly through objects and walls."
- The Witcher 3: "the game has a process that makes objects close to the camera appear transparent"
- Super Mario Sunshine: "A dark silhouette shines through the wall so that the player doesn't lose sight of the main character."
- God of War (inside to outside transition): "In God of War this is represented by a "hard cut""
- "The camera can then return to its previous position."
