# Cinemachine Decollider (3.1)
https://docs.unity3d.com/Packages/com.unity.cinemachine@3.1/manual/CinemachineDecollider.html
Fetched 14 Nov 2026 (WebFetch, quoted passages)

- "Cinemachine Decollider is an extension for the Camera. It post-processes the final position of the CinemachineCamera to pull the camera out of colliding objects."
- "no attempt will be made to preserve the line of sight to the target. For that, please use CinemachineDeoccluder."
- Camera Radius: "Distance to maintain from any obstacle or terrain. Try to keep this value small for the best results."
- Terrain Resolution: "When enabled, will attempt to place the camera on top of terrain layers."
- Decollision: "When enabled, will attempt to push the camera out of intersecting objects."
- Damping: "How quickly to return the camera to its normal position when a position adjustment is no longer needed."
- Smoothing Time: "Minimum number of seconds to hold the camera at the nearest point to the target."
