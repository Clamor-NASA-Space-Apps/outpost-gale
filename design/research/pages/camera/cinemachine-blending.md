# Cinemachine blending between cameras (2.8 manual, 3.1 Brain)

Fetched 14 Nov 2026 (WebFetch, quoted passages)

- "Use the Default Blend property in Cinemachine Brain to specify blends between Virtual Cameras that do not have custom blends." Duration: "Duration (in seconds) of the blend."
- "For blends between specific Virtual Cameras, use the Custom Blends list in the Cinemachine Brain component."
- Cut: "Zero-length blend."
- Ease In Out: "S-shaped curve, giving a gentle and smooth transition."
- Hard In: "Ease out of the outgoing Virtual Camera, and accelerate into the incoming Virtual Camera."
- Linear: "Linear blend. mechanical-looking."
- (3.1) Default Blend: "The blend to use when you haven't explicitly defined a blend between two CinemachineCameras."
Note: neither page states a default duration; none claimed here.
