I have just made a mod for my self that allows to configure day and night length.
By default it is 24 real time minutes for a day, and 10 real time minutes for the night.
With my mod i have a config file, where i can override these settings.
I am now currently testing it with 3 hours for a day and 3 hours for the night, but it can be set to anything.
Its amazing :-) Love it now already.
It just scales the time, hence preserving the balance.
Means, you still have to eat one or two times a day, it just takes much longer now before you get hungry.

I am going to publish the mod on nexusmods when im happy with tests. But if you want, i can share with you in advance so you can participate in testing.
