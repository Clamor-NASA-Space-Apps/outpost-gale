    During a 120 day dust storm, we assume no ISRU
production. Based on MER-B dust storm data [4]–[7], the
optical depth goes from the baseline 1.0 to a worse case value
of ~5.0. The incident energy on the solar array would be 35%
of the baseline design used in sizing. The solar arrays provide
power only to housekeeping loads during the dust storm. The
duration and intensity of most dust storms are not expected to
be as severe as the worst case scenario estimates used in this
study. In practice, the system would be placed into the dust
storm standby mode when daily solar intensity flux reaches a
set minimum point. The system would go into full production
mode when the daily flux increases above that point.
