       Electrodynamic Dust Shield: Masuda and col-
  laborators at the University of Tokyo developed a
 method to lift and transport particles called the elec-
 tric curtain, in which a series of parallel electrodes
 connected to an AC source generate a traveling wave
 that acts as a contactiess conveyor [5-8].
      We have developed an Electrodynamic Dust Shield
 prototype to remove dust from surfaces using elec-
 trodes that are alternately connected to an AC source
 and to ground. The electrodes are embedded in a
 transparent dielectric film to decrease their breakdown
 potential. Several prototype shields have been tested to
 check the cleaning efficiency as a function of various
 line widths and spacings. We used square waveforms
 in which the frequency was varied from 0 to 500 I-[z
 and the voltage was varied from 0 to 10 kV [9].
