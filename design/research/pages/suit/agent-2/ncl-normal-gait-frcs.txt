         Muscles - Histology
• Type 1 fibres
  – Myoglobin++ (red)
  – Weak/slow contraction, but not fatigable
• Type 2b fibres
  – Glycolytic enzyms++ (white)
  – Strong/fast contraction, but fatigable
• Type 2a fibres (between 1 and 2b)
        Muscle - Contraction
• Concentric contraction:
  – Shortening muscle length
  – Positive work
• Eccentric contraction:
  – Lengthening muscle length
  – Negative work

          More definitions…
• Concentric = Energy lost
  – Joint motion in the same direction than the
    internal motion
  – Muscle activity with shortening


• Eccentric = Energy absorbed
  – Joint motion opposed to the internal motion
  – Muscle activity with lengthening
