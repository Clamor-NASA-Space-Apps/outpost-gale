# Things that move at Gale and on Mars (excerpts)
Fetched 2026-11-12 with curl unless noted. Quotes verbatim.

## Dust devils at Gale
- NASA image article, sol 1597 (1 Feb 2017): https://www.nasa.gov/image-article/martian-dust-devil-action-gale-crater-sol-1597/
  - "a dust-carrying whirlwind, called a dust devil, scooting across the ground inside Gale Crater, as observed on the local summer afternoon"
  - "In the first and fifth frames, dust blowing across the ground appears as pale horizontal streak."
  - "dust devils are whirlwinds that result from sunshine warming the ground, prompting convective rising of air that has gained heat from the ground."
- PIA21483, sol 1613 (18 Feb 2017): https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/swirling-dust-in-gale-crater-mars-sol-1613/ ("on lower Mount Sharp inside Gale Crater ... during the summer afternoon").
- Search summary only (BGR, Aug 2020 dust devil, not saved): about 5 m wide, at least 50 m tall, imaged over 4 min 15 s. Treat as unverified.

## Sand ripples move, slowly (sol to sol, not second to second)
- NASA blog, "Sols 2864-2866: Spot the Difference!": https://science.nasa.gov/blog/sols-2864-2866-spot-the-difference/
  - "we're in the 'windy season' in Gale crater"
  - "We've been able to see the ripples moving from sol to sol, due to wind moving the sand grains that make up the ripples"
  - "Stronger surface heating tends to produce stronger convection and convective vortices ... If those vortices are strong enough, they can raise dust from the surface and become visible as 'dust devils'"
  - the Navcam movie covered "a period of about five minutes" and "you can just see it moving to the right ... even in the raw images."
