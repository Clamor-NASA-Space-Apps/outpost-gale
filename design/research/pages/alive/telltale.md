# Phoenix Telltale: how far Mars wind tilts it (12 Nov 2026, for data/world.json `telltale`)

- Phoenix help page (Washington Univ. analyst notebook), https://an.rsl.wustl.edu/phx2008/help/telltale.htm (fetched):
  "a lightweight cylinder" "suspended by Kevlar fibers"; wind 1 to 10 m/s across the camera's line of sight,
  1 m/s accuracy for 1 to 5 m/s, 20% for 5 to 10 m/s.
- Telltale Calibration Report, PDS bundle tt_bundle_1000, https://pds.nasa.gov/data/pds4/1000/tt_bundle_1000/document/TELLTALECALRPT.PDF
  (downloaded, 60 pages). Fig. 25, "Average deflection of the Telltale ... for different pressures", wind
  tunnel. Read off the 8 mbar curve by eye (page 28): 2 m/s about 0.005 rad, 4 m/s 0.02, 5 m/s 0.03,
  6.3 m/s 0.06, 7.7 m/s 0.085, 9.5 m/s 0.13, 11 m/s 0.19. At 20 mbar 12 m/s reaches about 0.42 rad.
  Telltale length L, from the images: "We find the mean L distance to be 28.1(2) mm."
- Search summaries only (not opened): a Kapton tube on Kevlar fibres, active mass about 10 mg (EGU2008-A-11861);
  mounted on a gallows on top of the meteorological mast (Aarhus University Telltale project page).
